{{ metadata.name }} is an open source news outlet, meaning anyone can contribute articles!

In particular, we are looking for Geometry Dash related news, guides, and stories, specifically targeted towards new players.

Our mission is to help out new players while showing them the potential the game has!

# Why Write For {{ metadata.name }}

Unfortunately, we cannot pay authors. In the future, we may look into creating a monetization program for frequent authors, but that isn't something we have the budget for right now.

So ideally, you write out of passion!

# Topic Recommendations

We recommend writing articles for any of the following categories:

- [2.21](/categories/2.2/) — Write about recent Geometry Dash 2.2 news or theories about the update
- [News](/categories/news/) — Write about the latest events and dramas happening in the Geometry Dash community
- [Demon List](/categories/news/) — Write about the latest Demon List news or upcoming extreme demons
- [Guide](/categories/guide/) — Tutorials on how to install mods, get icons, unlock secret areas, or anything else

That being said, you can write about mostly anything you want so long as its related to Geometry Dash. But we are specifically looking for coverage on the above categories as they're most popular amongst our readers.

# Getting Started

To get started, check out our [Contribute to Dashword](/courses/contribute-to-dashword/) course. It goes over everything you need to know to write and submit an article for publication!
