# Application

This Privacy Policy only applies to this website.

# Contacting Us

If you contact us, we may receive additional information about you such as your name, email address, phone number, the contents of messages or attachments you send us, and any other information you provide.

# Log Files

We follow the standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit this website. These log files may include:

- Your internet protocol (IP) addresses
- Your browser
- Your Internet Service Provider (ISP)
- The current date and time
- Pages you've visited
- Number of clicks you've made

These are not linked to information that is personally identifiable. We use this information to monitor our website, analyze user behavior, ban users who abuse our services, and to identify and fix bugs or other issues that come up.

# Cookies and Local Storage

Our websites may use Cookies and/or Local Storage to store user preferences, track the pages they've visited, and customize their experience.

# Google DoubleClick DART Cookies

This website uses Google as a third party vendor. Google uses DART cookies to serve ads to users visiting this website via their history on this site and other sites in their network.

However, you can decline the use of DART cookies here: [https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

# Advertising Partners

This website serves ads from the Google Display Network (GDN), which serves ads to our users based on information they've collected and continue to collect about you from sites you've visited in their network, which include this website.

As such, Google's Privacy Policy applies on this website. Google's Privacy Policy can be found here: [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

# Google Analytics

This website uses [Google Analytics](https://marketingplatform.google.com/about/analytics/) to collect usage data. We use this data to learn how our users are using our websites, such as by seeing how many users are on our websites, where they're from geographically, and other information.

You can use the [Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout) to opt-out of Google Analytics.

For more information, please see:

- [Google's Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
- [Google's "Safeguarding your data" help page](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/6004245)

# Third Party Privacy Policies

This Privacy Policy only applies to this website and not any other websites, such as third party websites we may link to.

# Children's Information

We do not knowingly collect any Personally Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you think your child provided this kind of information to us, email us at [support@dashword.net](mailto:support@dashword.net) immediately so we can remove such information from our records.
