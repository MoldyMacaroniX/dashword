Now that you've written your article, it is time to submit it for publication!

First things first: we cannot publish every article that gets sent to us. We also cannot reply to every submission that we reject. In general, if you don't hear back from us within 30 days, you should assume that your article was rejected.

If you want more help getting your article up to standard, you can [join our Discord server](/discord/) and ask around!

# Submitting Your Article for Publication

Before doing anything else, make sure your article follows our [Style Guide](/courses/contribute-to-dashword/style-guide/) and [Google Docs template rules](/courses/contribute-to-dashword/template/). If it doesn't, it will most likely get rejected!

Now, when you are ready to submit your article for publication, send an email to [support@dashword.net](mailto:support@dashword.net) with your article in the form of a shared Google Doc link. Make sure we have permission to access it!

Also, make sure you've read and agree to our [Author Terms](/author-terms/), which outline how we are allowed to use your article. We will check with you that you've read and agree to it before publishing your article.
